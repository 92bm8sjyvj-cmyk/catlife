Privacy Policy

這個應用程式只用來管理 Threads 帳號 @project.cat.life 自己的貼文和留言。我們只讀取公開留言的內容和留言者的帳號名稱，用來回覆。不收集其他個人資料，不出售也不分享資料。留言相關資料最多保存 90 天後刪除。如有疑問或想刪除資料，請私訊 @project.cat.life。

This app is used only to manage the posts and replies of the Threads account @project.cat.life. We read only the content of public replies and the replier's username in order to respond. We collect no other personal data, and never sell or share data. Reply-related data is deleted after at most 90 days. For questions or data deletion, send a DM to @project.cat.life.
